Mastheads

Every masthead this library ships, in both themes at once: base, industry and midgard, each as the plain bar and as the --vivid --brand variant.

Each bar is an iframe, and it has to be. The token layer scopes both themes to :root — :root[data-theme="dark"] and a prefers-color-scheme block against :root — so a light and a dark palette cannot coexist in one document. Every other gallery page toggles the whole page for that reason. A frame gets its own <html>, which is the only way to put the two side by side and the reason this page exists rather than a section on components.html.

The masthead variants

The plain bar is inked with text.primary by the consumer, so no skin repaints it — do not put a fill under ink you do not own. The masthead variants own their ink through gradient.ink, which is what makes them skinnable: under either skin they collapse to that skin’s flat banner, because base’s teal–blue–violet ribbon belongs to neither palette.

Compare the two themes on the SKINNED rows before touching either band. In dark each one is its own page colour — Industry’s #0d0f10 is the last stop of its page ramp and midgard’s #0b0f14 the first stop of its own, 1.000:1 either way — so the accent hairline is the entire boundary there, at 15.06:1 and 9.23:1. In light the fill step is already 16.91:1 and 14.65:1 and that same 1px reads as decoration. It is not.
A mark on a masthead is capped by the ink that crosses it. Midgard’s node network runs at 38% of the gold in dark and 20% in light against a 53% ceiling; Industry’s glitch marks are capped at 35% where they cross the ink band (roughly y19–37) and run to 55% below it, where nothing is inked. Neither spans the width, so neither carries a promise about layout — which a full-strength yellow strip did, and is why that one was built, measured and removed.
