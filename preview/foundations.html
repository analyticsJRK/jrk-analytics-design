The token layer. Contrast figures below are measured live against the surface each token actually renders on — switch the theme in the nav and every number recomputes.

Surfaces & ink

Dark values are selected for the dark surface, not flipped from light. Body text clears 4.5:1 on both the card surface and the page plane — the validator checks both, because a token that passes on white can fail on the canvas behind it.

Accent & focus

The solid button fill is blue-500, not blue-450. 450 with white text measures 4.42:1 — under AA. That is the kind of near-miss a build gate catches and an eye does not.

Radius & elevation

Elevation is opt-in. A dashboard of many tiles reads calmer flat — shadow is for things that genuinely float. In dark mode the lightened surface does nearly all the work: against a true black page a drop shadow is invisible, so the grey tile itself is the elevation cue and the shadow only tightens the edge.
