Sheet

Spreadsheet-style report rendering for dense financial output — a year of months across, many years down, totals and growth on the right, a chart alongside. Frozen row numbers and column letters, like the workbook it mirrors. Scroll it sideways: the gutter, the year column, and the letter bar all stay put.

What was modernised

Hierarchy instead of uniform navy. In the workbook the title bar, the metadata strip and every section band are the same heavy fill, so nothing leads. Here the title and metadata keep the strong band, section headers step down to a tint, and metric headers step down again — the page now has an outline you can scan.
Gridlines are optional. A full grid of rules is most of what makes a spreadsheet feel like one. The row hairline already separates rows, so the rules are off by default and available for parity with the source workbook.
Sign first, color second. Variance and growth cells always print the signed number; the green/red only reinforces it. And the tone comes from the metric's inverted flag, not the sign — for delinquency or an expense variance, down is the good outcome.
Frozen panes are a screen affordance. They unfreeze under @media print, because a sticky label column printed over the data is worse than no freeze at all.

Markup contract

This is a CSS grid, not a <table> — one shared track list is what lets a single column bar align with every stacked block. Semantics therefore have to be declared. The letter bar and the number gutter are aria-hidden: they are a coordinate system, not data. 

<div class="jrk-sheet" role="grid" aria-label="AM Report — Summit at Red Rocks">

  <div class="jrk-sheet__colbar" aria-hidden="true">
    <span class="jrk-sheet__col"></span><span class="jrk-sheet__col">A</span> …
  </div>

  <div class="jrk-sheet__row" role="row">
    <div class="jrk-sheet__gutter" aria-hidden="true">5</div>
    <div class="jrk-sheet__cell jrk-sheet__cell--label" role="rowheader">2024</div>
    <div class="jrk-sheet__cell jrk-sheet__cell--num" role="gridcell">$506,212</div>
    …
  </div>
</div>

Change the report's shape by overriding --jrk-sheet-cols on .jrk-sheet — never per row, or the letter bar stops lining up with the data under it.
