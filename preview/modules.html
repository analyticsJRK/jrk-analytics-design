What changed, and why

Against the 45-card grid this replaces.

  1. Still a card grid, ~40% shorter. The card lost its icon chip, its category footnote and its chevron; the description clamps to two lines. 104px against ~130px, eight columns against six, and grouped — the whole catalogue is about one screen at 1920×1080 instead of five.
  2. A Table view, and it is the library's table. A real <table> inside .jrk-table-wrap, so the radius, the hairline, the resting shadow, the zebra, the frozen header and leading column and the 70vh cap all come from table.css — nothing is restated here but the column widths. It is also where the submodules live: a grid of tiles has no room for a second level, and a .jrk-menu popover cannot be used inside a tile because .jrk-card is overflow: clip and would cut it off.
  3. A clicked submodule spins until the next page arrives. .jrk-spinner--sm replaces the row's glyph in a fixed-size slot so nothing reflows, the row takes aria-busy, and the label becomes “Opening …”. That label is not decoration: under prefers-reduced-motion base.css zeroes animation-duration as well as transitions, so the spinner does not turn and the words are the only feedback left.
  4. The icon chip is gone. Three glyphs were shared across 45 modules, so the mark on a card told you nothing about which card it was. The category glyph moved to the group header, where it labels something true, and the name moved into the space — text.md semibold, the largest thing on the tile.
  5. Category became structure instead of a footnote. It was printed on all 45 cards and organised nothing. It is now the group header, the filter and the count, stated once per area.
  6. Two actions, two shapes. The old card fused a chevron that navigated with a corner glyph that toggled membership, so neither was safe to aim at. They are split and the split is visible in the geometry the library already uses: tiles are radius.md rectangles, controls are radius.full pills. The tile opens the module; the pill pins it to your portal. The pill is the one thing on the card that is not a rectangle.
  7. Hover lifts the tile 3px. More than emphasis: shadow.cardRaise is invisible in dark by construction, so the hover used to rest on a 1.125:1 fill step there. A transform is theme-blind — it is the first channel on this tile that reads the same in both.
  8. Submodules exist now. The search field always claimed to match them and the page never showed one. The footer carries the count, and a search that hits a submodule names the match on the card instead.
  9. In-portal sorts first inside each group — on load, not on toggle, so nothing jumps under the pointer. Each group header states its own n of m, and the filter counts follow the current search rather than restating a constant. With the accent edge and the In portal filter both removed, that n of m is the only thing left answering “what do I have” without reading every tile — it is not decoration.
